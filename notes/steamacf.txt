WHAT THIS BUYS, AND IT IS LESS THAN IT LOOKS
A total that closes to the byte says the tree WEIGHS what Steam
thinks it weighs. It says nothing about any individual file: one
byte changed anywhere leaves SizeOnDisk untouched, and so does any
pair of edits that cancel. It is not a checksum and must not be
reported as one.
